# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

The primary users are recruiters, hiring managers, and engineering leads evaluating Muhammad Afiq Apandi for full-stack software engineering roles. They need to understand his scope, evidence, and professional fit quickly, then reach a credible next step without reconstructing his experience from disconnected profiles.

## Product Purpose

This portfolio presents Afiq's verified professional experience, selected work, technical capabilities, education, and contact paths in one coherent place. It should help qualified visitors decide whether to start a professional conversation or interview. Success means the site makes his full-stack contribution legible, supports deeper review through the CV and dedicated pages, and generates relevant professional enquiries.

## Positioning

Afiq is positioned as a full-stack developer with evidence of end-to-end production delivery across React Native applications and serverless AWS systems. The portfolio distinguishes that position through concrete work outcomes, including a browser-based e-signature workflow and experience delivering and supporting more than 50 client websites, rather than through unsupported claims or a generic tool list.

## Operating Context

Visitors arrive from applications, referrals, search, or professional profiles and may review the site on desktop or mobile. The portfolio is intentionally multi-page: the homepage provides a concise overview, while About, Experience, Projects, and Skills provide focused detail. Visitors can download the public CV and continue through the published email, personal website, LinkedIn, and GitHub links.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The existing application is a Next.js and TypeScript website with responsive navigation, light and dark themes, and separate routes for About, Experience, Projects, and Skills.
- Preserve the full-stack positioning and multi-page information architecture.
- Personal, employment, education, project, metric, credential, and contact claims must remain grounded in the public CV or other user-confirmed evidence. Do not invent missing facts or imply unavailable proof.
- Keep specific work outcomes connected to their professional context and make the path from summary to supporting detail clear.
- The current public language is English.

## Brand Commitments

The public identity is Muhammad Afiq Apandi, presented as a Full-Stack Developer based in Cyberjaya, Malaysia. The voice is direct, professional, specific, and evidence-led. Preserve the published `afiq.dev`, LinkedIn, GitHub, and email destinations unless the user supplies replacements.

## Evidence on Hand

- `public/muhammad-afiq-apandi-cv.pdf` is the primary résumé evidence shipped with the site.
- `src/app/page.tsx`, `src/app/about/page.tsx`, `src/app/experience/page.tsx`, `src/app/projects/page.tsx`, and `src/app/skills/page.tsx` contain the current public portfolio narrative and supporting detail.
- The repository currently includes concrete employment, project, education, technology, and outcome statements. Future work must preserve their evidentiary basis and must not fabricate testimonials, clients, benchmarks, awards, or additional metrics.

## Product Principles

1. Evidence before embellishment: every professional claim should be supportable from confirmed source material.
2. Make full-stack scope obvious: connect interface work, backend services, cloud infrastructure, and delivery responsibility into one coherent account.
3. Lead with outcomes, then provide technical depth for visitors who want to inspect it.
4. Support fast evaluation without flattening the portfolio into a single page; summaries should link to focused detail.
5. Keep professional contact and CV access clear, trustworthy, and usable.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The website must meet WCAG 2.2 Level AA. Core content and navigation must remain operable by keyboard, understandable with semantic structure, readable at responsive sizes, and compatible with light and dark themes without relying on color alone.
